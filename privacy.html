Privacy policy

The app keeps what it uses on your iPhone and sends us nothing.

Last changed: 25 November 2026

In short

The Solpath app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests of its own.

What the app uses, and where it stays

Your location
Used to work out the sun where you are, to name a nearby city from the list inside the app, and to find true north. Solpath keeps it only in memory, does not send it, and does not store it unless you save it as a place.
The camera picture
Shown on the screen behind the sun’s path while the Camera tab is open. Solpath takes no photos, records no video or sound, and does not save or send the picture.
Compass and motion sensors
Used at that moment to know which way the phone points and how it is held. The readings are not stored or sent. The “Align to sun” adjustment is forgotten when you leave the Camera tab.
Device model and details
Solpath reads the model of your iPhone to choose the camera’s field of view. Parts the app is built with also read technical details when it starts, such as the device name and the iOS version; Solpath makes no use of them. None of this is stored or leaves your iPhone.
Time settings
Your iPhone’s time zone and its 12-hour or 24-hour setting, read to show times. They are not sent. When you save your current location, the time zone is kept with that place.
Saved places
The name, area, coordinates and time zone of each place you save, and which one is selected, are kept in a file in the app’s own storage on your iPhone. If that file is ever damaged, the damaged copy is kept beside it. We cannot see either.
App state
An empty file notes that you have seen the introduction, and iOS keeps a few layout settings for the app, such as the text direction. Nothing in them is about you.

Permissions

iOS asks you before Solpath can use your location or the camera. You can change both in the Settings app at any time. Without your location you can still choose a place from the city list.

Device backups

Saved places are part of the app’s data, so they can be included in your iCloud Backup or a backup you make on a computer. See what iCloud Backup includes. Apple’s terms apply to those backups.

Deleting your data

You can delete each place inside the app. Deleting the app deletes its files from your iPhone. Device backups have to be deleted where you keep them.

What iOS does

iOS finds your location for the app. Apple describes how in Location Services & Privacy.

As with every app, iOS takes a picture of the screen for the app switcher when you leave Solpath. Solpath does not use that picture.

This website

“Privacy” and “Support” in the app’s Settings open this website in your browser. The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, not us, logs and stores the IP address of visitors for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. See GitHub’s explanation and the GitHub privacy statement.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you.

What you write on GitHub Issues is public. Please do not include personal details or your location.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new text appears on this page with a new date. For questions, email appfab.apps@outlook.com.
