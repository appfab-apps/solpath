Where the sun goes, from where you stand.

Solpath is an iPhone app that shows the sun’s path today, or on any other day, for the place you are in or a place you choose.

The sun on a compass

A dial that turns with your phone shows the sun’s path across the day, where the sun is now and which way shadows fall.

The day in hours

Sunrise, sunset, solar noon, twilight and golden hour, with the length of the day and how it changes.

Any date, any hour

Slide through the day or pick a date, and see where the sun will be.

Your places

Save the spots you care about, or find a city in the list inside the app. Times are shown in each place’s own time zone.

The path in the camera

Hold your phone upright and see the sun’s path drawn over the camera picture.

No signal needed

Everything is worked out on your iPhone. The app makes no network requests.

Never look directly at the sun

Looking at the sun can damage your eyes. Look only at your phone’s screen, also when you use the camera.

Your privacy

Solpath has no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking. Your location and your saved places stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy, or find help on the support page.
